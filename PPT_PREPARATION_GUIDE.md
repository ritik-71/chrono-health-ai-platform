# ChronoHealth AI - PPT Presentation Structure Guide

## Suggested Slide Flow (12-15 Slides)

### Slide 1: Title Slide
- Project Name: ChronoHealth AI Platform
- Subtitle: Adaptive AI/ML for Circadian Optimization
- Presenter Name & Logo

### Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Rise of chronic stress and sleep disorders.
- Limitations of current "Static" healthcare monitoring.
- Lack of "Explainability" in medical AI models.

### Slide 3: Proposed Solution
- A dynamic, AI-driven longitudinal dashboard.
- Integration of ML, RL, and XAI (Explainable AI).
- Real-time clinical feedback loop.

### Slide 4: System Architecture
- Diagram showing Next.js Frontend ↔ FastAPI Backend ↔ PostgreSQL Database.
- Highlight the decoupled ML/RL Inference Layer.

### Slide 5: Tech Stack
- Icons/Lists: Next.js, FastAPI, XGBoost, SHAP, Q-Learning, Neon.

### Slide 6: The CII Engine (Innovation)
- Explanation of the proprietary Circadian Interaction Index formula.
- How it bridges Stress, Sleep, and Environmental factors.

### Slide 7: AI/ML Modules (Prediction)
- Stress Predictor, Sleep Analysis, and Fatigue tracking.
- Show a screenshot of the Radar and Line charts.

### Slide 8: Explainable AI (SHAP)
- Why clinicians need "Why".
- Show a waterfall plot and explain feature contributions.

### Slide 9: Reinforcement Learning (RL)
- Personalizing interventions.
- Explain the Q-Table and Reward feedback system.

### Slide 10: The Upload & Data Pipeline
- Automated CSV/JSON ingestion.
- Database persistence and dashboard recomputation.

### Slide 11: Deployment Architecture
- Vercel + Render + Neon integration.
- Production stability and Dockerization.

### Slide 12: Future Scope
- Wearable device integration (Apple Watch/Garmin).
- Real-time HIPAA-compliant cloud scaling.
- Predictive PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) RL models.

### Slide 13: Conclusion & Key Takeaways
- Investor-ready, clinically-validated platform.
- Scalable architecture for future healthcare SaaS.
