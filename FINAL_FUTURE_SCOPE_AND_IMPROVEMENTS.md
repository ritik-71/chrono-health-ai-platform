# ChronoHealth AI Platform — Future Scope & Improvements

## 🛰️ Real-Time IoT Integration
*   **Direct Sync**: Move from CSV uploads to direct API streaming from Oura, Whoop, and Apple Health.
*   **Edge Inference**: Run lightweight prediction models directly on mobile devices to reduce latency.

## 🧬 Genetic Stratification
*   **Chronotypes**: Integrate polygenic risk scores (PRS) to identify "Night Owls" vs. "Morning Larks" with higher precision.
*   **Pharmacogenomics**: Suggest optimal medication timing based on personal circadian profiles.

## 🔒 Security & Compliance
*   **HIPAA Compliance**: Transition to encrypted, zero-knowledge storage for patient identifiable information.
*   **Audit Trails**: Implement immutable logs for all clinical decision support actions.

## 🤖 Advanced Multi-Agent RL
*   **Collaborative AI**: Implement multiple RL agents that coordinate between sleep, diet, and exercise to find the global optimum for patient health.
