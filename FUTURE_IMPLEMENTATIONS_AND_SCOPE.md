# ChronoHealth AI — Future Implementations & Scope

## 1. Technical Roadmap

### Real-time Wearable Integration
- **Biometric Streams**: Direct API integration with Apple HealthKit, Google Fit, and Oura.
- **Streaming Sockets**: Transition from 10s polling to WebSocket-based real-time data streaming.
- **On-Device Inference**: Moving lightweight stress classifiers to the edge (mobile/wearable) for immediate alerts.

### Advanced Reinforcement Learning
- **Deep Q-Networks (DQN)**: Moving from table-based Q-learning to neural-network-based RL for high-dimensional state spaces.
- **Multi-Agent Systems**: Coordinating multiple RL agents for group-based chronobiology (e.g., night-shift worker teams).

## 2. Clinical Expansion

### Multimodal AI
- **Vision Integration**: Analyzing facial fatigue markers and pupil dilation via smartphone cameras.
- **Voice Biomarkers**: Detecting mental fatigue and stress through acoustic speech analysis.

### Mental Health Forecasting
- **Proactive Intervention**: Using longitudinal CII trends to predict burnout or depressive episodes up to 7 days in advance.
- **Comorbidity Tracking**: Expanding models to include anxiety and cardiovascular health correlations.

## 3. Enterprise & SaaS Scaling

### Clinician Dashboards
- **Multi-Tenant Portal**: A centralized interface for healthcare providers to monitor hundreds of patients simultaneously.
- **Automated Reporting**: Generation of PDF clinical summaries for insurance and medical records.

### Hospital & EHR Integration
- **HL7/FHIR Support**: Ensuring compatibility with electronic health records (Epic, Cerner) for seamless data portability.
- **Federated Learning**: Training models on decentralized hospital data to ensure privacy while improving global AI accuracy.

## 4. Vision for 2026
ChronoHealth AI aims to become the industry standard for **Chronobiology-as-a-Service**, providing specialized clinical intelligence to researchers, hospitals, and high-performance teams worldwide.
